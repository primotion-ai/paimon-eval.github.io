What each metric measures

Within-trajectory agreement

For fixed pairs of frames from one trajectory, we compare the model’s progress ordering (<, ≈, >) with the benchmark labels. A difference of at most 0.1 is a tie. The score is the percentage of correctly matched relations.

Cross-trajectory agreement

The same relation test is applied to pairs of frames from different trajectories, checking whether progress is comparable across executions. Results cover S ↔ S, N ↔ N, and S ↔ N pairs.

Terminal outcome agreement

The last-frame progress predicts failure [0, 0.33), suboptimal execution [0.33, 0.66), or success [0.66, 1]. The score is the percentage of trajectories whose predicted outcome matches the benchmark annotation.

Mean absolute error

MAE = 100 × mean |model progress − benchmark progress| over all frames, with equal weight per frame. It measures absolute calibration in percentage points; lower is better. No tie tolerance is applied.
